A Quality of Life mod that adds various configurable adjustments and additions to provide information and convenience.
ALL features of this mod can be toggled in the Config menu.

Some features include:
- Adds several town NPCs, selling rare enemy spawn boosters, boss summons and event starters
- Helpful extra tooltips, for example showing which items are sold by which NPCs, and special Shimmer recipes
- Infinite ammos, potions, etc at high enough stack
- Craftable instant builds for common world infrastructure like long platforms and fishing lakes 
- Powerful explosives for clearing out large parts of the world
- Spawn rate increase/decrease items

And much more!
